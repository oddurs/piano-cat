# Where the notes came from

Every file in this directory is generated by `tools/import-kern.mjs` or
`tools/import-midi.mjs` from a published digital edition. Nothing here is
typed from memory, and the command that produced each file is written at the
top of it, so any of them can be rebuilt and checked.

The music is all public domain — the most recent composer here died in 1917.
The **digital editions** are the work of the people below, and they are why
these transcriptions are complete and correct rather than approximately right
for the first eight bars.

## Not from an edition

**Chopsticks** is a folk piece with no urtext — Euphemia Allen's 1877 *Celebrated
Chop Waltz* is the ancestor of it, but what everybody actually plays is an oral
tradition. Its notes are hand-written in `lib/pieces.ts` and labelled as an
arrangement rather than an edition, because that is what they are.

## A note on what is generated

What these files contain is pitch and duration data for public-domain music.
They were read from the editions above, and those editors are credited here
because the work of making an accurate digital edition is real work and this
project is standing on it. If you maintain one of these and would rather this
project sourced its notes differently, please open an issue.
