# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be kind. It's a cat that plays piano.

## The longer version

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant][cc]. In practice:

**Expected:** treat people with respect, assume good faith, accept that
reasonable people disagree about timing constants, and give feedback on the
work rather than the person.

**Not expected:** harassment of any kind, personal or political attacks,
demeaning or discriminatory comments, publishing anyone's private information,
or sustained disruption of discussions.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages and anywhere else the
project is being represented.

## Enforcement

Report anything that needs attention to <oddurs@gmail.com>. Reports are handled
privately and in confidence.

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, close issues and pull
requests, or block accounts, in response to behaviour judged inappropriate —
and will explain the decision where it is reasonable to do so.

There is no committee here; this is one person maintaining a toy in their spare
time. The bar for action is simply whether the project stays a pleasant place
to spend an afternoon.

[cc]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
