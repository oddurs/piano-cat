# Piano samples

These 30 `.mp3` files are from the **Salamander Grand Piano V3** by
**Alexander Holm**, released under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
licence.

They were fetched from the Tone.js sample mirror
(`https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/`), which redistributes the same
recordings as mp3.

They are vendored into this repository on purpose: the app should work offline
and must not depend on a third-party CDN staying up. One sample every minor
third from A0 to C8; everything in between is pitch-shifted at playback.

If you reuse them, keep the attribution.
